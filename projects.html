Famous Artists Analysis

Using SQL, Excel, and Power BI, I explored trends, comparisons, and key insights, analysing artist distribution, museum collections, artwork pricing behaviour, and the popularity of different painting subjects. I performed multi-table joins, aggregations, filtering, CASE statements, and subqueries to examine relationships between artists, artworks, museums, and pricing data, identifying insights such as the most common artwork subjects, museum pricing patterns, and artwork distribution across collections. Using Excel and Power BI, I created charts, pivot tables, and interactive dashboards to present trends and findings through data visualisation and storytelling. This project further strengthened my skills in SQL querying, data analysis, data visualisation, and working with larger multi-table relational datasets, while also improving my experience with Power BI dashboard development and analytical reporting.

World Population Analysis

A real-world global population dataset was explored, containing detailed country-level information such as population across multiple years, land area, density, growth rate, and overall world population share. The analysis was carried out using Pandas and Python, with a focus on data preparation, exploratory analysis, and uncovering underlying patterns within the dataset. To support the analysis, Matplotlib, Seaborn, and Plotly were used to create visualisations including bar charts, histograms, heatmaps, pie charts, and a world choropleth map, helping to present insights clearly and intuitively. Overall, the project demonstrates structured data handling, insight discovery, and effective visual communication of global population trends. All work is documented within a Jupyter Notebook.
